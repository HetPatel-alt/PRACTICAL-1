Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for common StudentHub questions.

General questions

Who can use StudentHub?

Students and authorised university staff can use the portal. Account features may depend on the user's role.

Academic questions

Where is my attendance?

Open your Dashboard to see the attendance information available for your courses.

Marksheet questions

How do I get my marksheet?

Check the Dashboard for result announcements and follow the university's official process for published results.

Events questions

Can I register for an event?

Open Events, select an event and use its registration link. Sign-in may be required.
